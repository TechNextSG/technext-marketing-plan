The ads work. The setup doesn't.

Two Facebook ads are live and buying attention cheaply. But both are set to Traffic, so nothing counts a lead — which is why $223 of spend has produced no idea what an enquiry costs.

The proposal: $50 a day for 8 weeks — $3,000. One ad, one ad set, objective switched to Leads, lead tracking turned on. That should produce roughly 100 leads, 20 demos and 1–2 clients, at around $1,500–3,000 of media per client against a first-year contract near SGD 13,000.

2 Four fixes

The first three are free and matter more than the budget.

1

Track the lead

Fire Lead on the /lp/ confirmation only — pixel plus Conversions API. Submit a real test lead and confirm a human is notified.

2

Pause the loser

Stop "5 Different Spreadsheets". Put everything behind the $0.25 ad. Keep the paused one as a challenger for later.

3

Clear the drafts

Ads Manager shows 8 unpublished drafts. Publish deliberately or discard — that is how budget gets spent by accident.

4

Switch to Leads

Same creative, same audience, same /lp/ destination. Objective from Traffic to Leads. Five-minute edit, and the only one that turns spend into pipeline.

Nothing needs designing or writing. technext.asia/lp/ is already a proper Odoo landing page with a five-field form and a privacy link. The creative's old missing-s typo is fixed. Both ads carry the correct Odoo Ready Partner badge. This is a settings problem, not a content problem.

Why 8 weeks, and why not less. Meta wants around 50 conversions per ad set per week to deliver predictably. At $31 a lead that would cost roughly $6,000 a month — not realistic here, so this ad set will run in extended learning and its weekly numbers will bounce around. The answer is time, not money: one ad set so all the data lands in one place, and eight weeks before judging. Stopping at week 3 writes off the spend that buys the answer.
⚠ Confirm the account currency first. Ads Manager shows a bare $ for this account. Every figure here is quoted as the platform displays it. If it bills in USD, $50/day is about SGD 67/day — check Billing → Payment methods and restate in one currency before this goes for approval. The balance is also down to $1.99 and needs topping up.
