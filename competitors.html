The pattern across every company. Partners buy Google Search text ads first — it is the main or only format at Captivea, Serpent, SGEEDE, Applivon and Alitec, and half of Omnisoft's set. Display banners are the second format (Port Cities, Neu Media, HashMicro). Video is a side dish everywhere except HashMicro. On Facebook, the only Odoo advertisers in Singapore are Odoo itself, two Indonesian firms, a lead-magnet site — and us. On LinkedIn, only Odoo and Neu Media spend.

Two limits to keep in mind. Ad counts are live creatives, not spend — but a company with 200 creatives is running a programme and a company with zero is running nothing.
