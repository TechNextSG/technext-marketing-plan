Where Odoo partners advertise.

All 19 companies in Odoo's Singapore partner directory, checked one by one against the public Google, Meta and LinkedIn ad libraries on 24 Aug 2026 — plus the vendor itself and its loudest rival.

The finding: this market advertises on Google Search, and almost nowhere else. Seven of eighteen competitor partners run Google ads — nearly all of them search text ads. On Facebook, the entire country shows about 14 active ads that mention Odoo, and not one comes from a Singapore partner — two of the 14 are ours. The Meta plan on page 2 runs where no local competitor is bidding.

2 What they run

One format dominates, and the messages repeat.

1

Search text ads are the default

Every advertising partner leads with Google Search: Captivea is 100% search, Alitec and Omnisoft are search-first, and Odoo's own ~200 Singapore ads are practically all text. The whole niche is bidding on the same bottom-of-funnel keywords — with the vendor itself in the auction.

2

Port Cities is the display exception

~200 image banners and zero video — a remarketing blanket that follows visitors around the web. It is the only partner spending real money on staying visible after the click.

3

Video is rare

Three video ads at Omnisoft, one at Alitec, none at Port Cities or Captivea. Only HashMicro funds video at scale (~300). Nobody expects to win this market on YouTube.

4

Grants are the favourite hook

Alitec's live ad lands on alitec.sg/odoo/ctc_grant; Port Cities leads its site and ads with PSG-vendor status. "The government pays for most of it" is the message this market believes converts — ahead of any product feature.

This is the strongest argument for the page 2 plan. Every competitor that pays for attention buys it on Google Search, where the vendor itself and HashMicro drive prices up. On Facebook the Odoo conversation in Singapore is empty — our two ads compete with generic SME advertisers for attention, not with ERP rivals. Being early here is the whole point; it will not stay empty forever.

LinkedIn is a smaller version of the same picture: 48 Odoo-related ads shown in Singapore, almost all from Odoo itself (report downloads, event invitations) and from Indian partners selling globally. The one local name is Neu Media, which promotes its webinars as LinkedIn events. No Gold or Silver Singapore partner spends there.

4 What we take from it

Four conclusions, each following from the tables above.

1

Hold the Meta plan

Zero local partners on Facebook confirms the channel choice on page 2. $1,000 a month buys uncontested Odoo attention there; the same money on Google Search buys an auction against Odoo, HashMicro and every Gold partner.

2

If we ever test search, lead with grants

The partners with money on the table bet on grant messaging, not features. A future search test should land on a CTC/PSG-angle page, the way Alitec's does — after checking which grants genuinely apply to us.

3

Ride Odoo's own event spend

The biggest Meta budget in this niche is Odoo promoting its Singapore Business Show. That audience is bought for us — attending and working the event costs a ticket, not a media budget.

4

Expect prospects pre-sold by HashMicro

At ~2,000 ads region-wide it out-advertises every partner combined, so prospects will have seen ERP ads before ours — usually HashMicro's. The discovery call should assume that comparison is coming and be ready for it.

How this was measured. Counts read from the Google Ads Transparency Center (ads shown in Singapore, any time), the Meta Ad Library (active ads, Singapore) and the LinkedIn Ad Library on 24 Aug 2026. All three are public. Ad counts are not spend — but a partner with 200 live creatives is running a programme, and a partner with zero is running nothing. Worth re-reading quarterly; it takes an hour.
