1 Company by company

Ordered by ad volume. Counts are live creatives in each library, filtered to the company's own verified advertiser account. "In SG" = ads shown in Singapore.

Odoo the vendor · advertiser "OpenERP SA" Google Search YouTube Facebook Instagram LinkedIn ~200 in SG on Google — ~all search text, 2 video. 5 active Meta ads. Heavy LinkedIn presence. Search ads on product keywords; Meta image/video ads pushing free sign-ups to its Singapore Business Show and manufacturing offers; LinkedIn report downloads, event invites and sponsored InMail.

Search ads on "ERP solutions Singapore" plus sitelink-heavy brand ads ("Trusted Partner", pricing plans) and a few YouTube videos ("Odoo — Level Up").
Alitec Gold Google Search Google Display YouTube 5 on Google (2 search text, 2 display image, 1 video); 4 shown in SG. No Meta, no LinkedIn. Small always-on set; the live search ad ("Management software") lands on its CTC-grant page — grant funding is the pitch.
SG-Soft / SGEEDE Silver · pays via PT SGEEDE, Indonesia Google Search Facebook 5 on Google — all search text, 2 shown in SG. 1 active Meta ad delivering into SG. Indonesian-market ads that spill into Singapore: Bahasa-language Facebook ad offering a free consultation (lists an SG phone number), plus a few search ads.
Applivon Ready Google Search 1 search text ad, shown in SG. A single ad — token presence.
ICS Compute not a directory partner · Indonesian cloud firm Facebook Instagram 3 active Meta ads delivering into SG. Nothing on Google for SG. Bahasa-language image ads selling Odoo Enterprise packages (licence + hosting + implementation), "Contact us" objective.
SmarterWay.AI not a partner — ERP comparison site Facebook Instagram 1 active Meta ad (2 versions) in SG. Lead-magnet ad: "Compare 20+ ERP platforms" guide behind a registration form, "Download" objective. Collects the same leads everyone here wants.
TechNext for reference — us Facebook Instagram 2 active Meta ads in SG. The only Singapore directory partner with any Meta ads. The two image ads from page 2 — free consultation / workflow review, "Book now".
The pattern across every company. Partners buy Google Search text ads first — it is the main or only format at Captivea, Serpent, SGEEDE, Applivon and Alitec, and half of Omnisoft's set. Display banners are the second format (Port Cities, Neu Media, HashMicro). Video is a side dish everywhere except HashMicro. On Facebook, the only Odoo advertisers in Singapore are Odoo itself, two Indonesian firms, a lead-magnet site — and us. On LinkedIn, only Odoo and Neu Media spend.

Two limits to keep in mind. Ad counts are live creatives, not spend — but a company with 200 creatives is running a programme and a company with zero is running nothing. And Meta's library only lists active ads for regular advertisers, so a competitor between campaigns shows as empty. Checked 24 Aug 2026; worth re-reading quarterly — it takes an hour.
