Email marketing, Singapore.

Send 11 researched emails a day to Singapore SMEs about their IRAS InvoiceNow deadline. That is the plan. Everything below is detail.

Market Singapore Sends from technextasia.com Cold cap 11/day Term 90 days

1 The plan

Four decisions.

1

Send from technextasia.com

SPF, DKIM and DMARC all pass on it. The look-alike technext.asia is the website and has no SPF — nothing sends from there.

2

Lead with InvoiceNow

IRAS is phasing in mandatory e-invoicing on fixed dates by turnover band. Almost no SME knows their own date. Better hook than anything about us.

3

Cap cold at 11 a day

Singapore's Spam Control Act adds an <ADV> subject prefix once unsolicited mail goes out in bulk — over 100 in 24h, 1,000 in 30 days or 10,000 a year. 11/day stays under all three, so subject lines stay clean.

4

Research the list, don't scrape it

First deliverable is 120 rows, each with a named contact and a trigger we can check in under two minutes. Quality here sets the reply rate for everything after.

Two lanes, never mixed. Cold goes out small and plain from Google Workspace. Volume goes only to people who opted in, through Brevo. That is why building an opted-in list is a deliverable in its own right and not a by-product — without one, the bulk-send capacity cannot lawfully be used.

For lane 2, the three HTML templates are already built and live at technextsg.github.io/technext-email-marketing. No new design work — Singapore copy only. Monthly newsletter, second week, Tuesday morning SGT.

⚠ Two things the copy must never do.
  • Never state a specific InvoiceNow date for a specific company. Say "your date depends on your turnover band, and we confirm it in scoping."
  • Never imply grant funding. PSG/EDG pre-approval is unverified. Say "we can point you to the current schemes" and nothing more.
